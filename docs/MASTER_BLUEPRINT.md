## Document Purpose

This Master Blueprint is the single source of truth for building Nyaya Revolution from vision through engineering execution. It defines the product strategy, user experience, content architecture, business model, technical foundation, and operational roadmap required to launch and scale India's first situation-based legal learning platform.

Intended audience: founders, product, engineering, content, legal, design, growth, and investor stakeholders.
